STUDENT ORGANIZERS' CODE OF CONDUCT & EVENT RULES:

1. ZERO TOLERANCE FOR ALCOHOL & INTOXICANTS:
Bringing, consuming, distributing, or entering under the influence of alcohol, drugs, narcotics, smoking, or any intoxicant is strictly forbidden. Any violation will result in immediate removal, forfeiture of pass without refund, and disciplinary action by organizing committee.
2. RESPECTFUL BEHAVIOR & DECORUM:
Attendees must maintain high personal and professional conduct. Treat all juniors, seniors, and service staff with respect. Healthy interaction and positive celebration are expected.

Ragging, eve-teasing, harassment (verbal, physical, or non-verbal), inappropriate comments, or physical intimidation are strictly prohibited. Violators will face immediate pass cancellation, venue eviction, and disciplinary action by organizing committee.

Photography is permitted in designated stages, photo booths, and common event spaces. Unconsented, intrusive, or candid photography/videography of fellow students is strictly prohibited.
